FAQ

Before You Ask

How do we start?

Message me on WhatsApp with your idea or your raw footage, plus one or two videos you like as references. I'll reply with a plan and a fixed quote — work only begins when you approve it.

What do I need to send you?

For editing: your footage (Google Drive link is fine) and any brand assets. For faceless production: just your niche and channel style — I handle script, voice, edit and thumbnail.

How fast is delivery?

Shorts are typically delivered within 48 hours. Long-form projects get an agreed deadline before we start. Rush delivery is available — ask on WhatsApp.

How many revisions are included?

Two full revision rounds are included in every package. Because we lock the structure first, most projects need only one.

How do I pay?

International payments via Payoneer. On first projects we usually start with 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, so both sides are protected.

Do you offer monthly packages?

Yes — channel retainers with a fixed number of Shorts or videos per month at a better per-video rate, with priority turnaround. Tell me your posting schedule on WhatsApp and I'll build a package around it.

Which niches do you work in?

Money & psychology, education, kids' content, fitness, tech explainers and documentary-style channels. If your niche isn't listed, send me a sample video — if I can't do it justice, I'll tell you honestly.
