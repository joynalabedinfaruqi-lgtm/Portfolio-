01 The Framework

What Goes Into Every Video

Three things, in every single video I ship. It is the exact system behind my own channel's daily Shorts — tested in public, refined every day.

01
🪝

Stop

The first 3 seconds decide everything. Every video opens with its biggest claim or question — no intros, no logos, no warm-up. If the scroll doesn't stop, nothing else matters.

02
🎬

Watch

A story arc in under a minute: tension, payoff, one idea at a time. Word-by-word kinetic captions and sound design keep eyes locked until the final frame.

03
🔁

Follow

Every ending loops back to the topic and gives one clear reason to follow — never a generic "follow for more". The viewer leaves wanting the next video, not just this one.

03 How It Works

From Brief To Published

Six steps, followed in every project — you always know exactly where your video is.

01

Brief

A few quick questions on WhatsApp: your niche, your goal, 1–2 reference videos. You get a plan and a fixed quote before anything starts.

02

Hook & Script

The hook is written first — the biggest claim or question in line one — then the full script is built around it and locked with you.

03

Voice & Visuals

Voiceover recorded or produced, scenes and visuals planned shot-by-shot so the edit never hunts for material.

04

Edit & Motion

The cut, motion graphics, counters and kinetic type — assembled for retention, not decoration.

05

Captions & Sound

Word-by-word captions synced to the voice, music bed and frame-synced sound effects. Then your two revision rounds.

06

Thumbnail & Delivery

A bold RGB thumbnail with one clear idea, plus upload-ready files in every format you need — ready to post.

07 FAQ

Before You Ask

01 How do we start?+

Message me on WhatsApp with your idea or your raw footage, plus one or two videos you like as references. I'll reply with a plan and a fixed quote — work only begins when you approve it.

02 What do I need to send you?+

For editing: your footage (Google Drive link is fine) and any brand assets. For faceless production: just your niche and channel style — I handle script, voice, edit and thumbnail.

03 How fast is delivery?+

Shorts are typically delivered within 48 hours. Monthly packages run on a rolling schedule agreed in advance. Rush delivery is available — ask on WhatsApp.

04 How many revisions are included?+

Two full revision rounds are included in every package. Because we lock the hook and script first, most projects need only one.

05 Who owns the final files?+

You do — completely, the moment they're delivered: final videos, thumbnails and project files on request.

06 How do I pay?+

International payments via Payoneer. On first projects we usually start with 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, so both sides are protected.
