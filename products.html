About the studio

Cognitive Wallet is a one-person design studio run by Joynal Abedin Faruqi, a professional video editor and motion designer. The studio also produces the Cognitive Wallet YouTube channel, which explains the psychology of money in short daily videos. These printable products take the same ideas off the screen and onto paper, where people can use them every day. All products are original designs, made in-house, and every purchase includes both US Letter and A4 sizes.
